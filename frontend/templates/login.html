Security Advisory

Never expose Sniparr — or any Docker container — directly to the internet. Always access your self-hosted services through a secure private network tunnel such as Tailscale, WireGuard, or a hardened reverse proxy with authentication. Leaving management ports open publicly puts your entire media stack at risk.
